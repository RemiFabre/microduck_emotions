Microduck emotions

Fourteen emotions for Microduck. Each one is a motion and a sound designed together, beat by beat, and the beak opens with the sound. All fourteen are on the robot's gamepad. These clips are rendered in MuJoCo with the robot's own control policies, its servo model and the same sound files the robot plays.

The order tells a small story so people keep watching. It opens on the strongest moment and ends on a punchline. The camera never stops: each shot starts at the angle where the last one ended, so the duck seems to change mood in one continuous take. Click a time to jump there.

  1. The hook

    A shock call and a sudden collapse in the first second, then three sobs. A robot crying makes people stay. Cut before it gets up.

  2. The snap

    From tragedy straight to "what? what?". The tonal jump is the joke.

  3. Quick-fire answers

    Three short shots of 1.25 to 1.6 s. The cutting speeds up.

  4. The build-up

    Irritation that climbs until the duck barks.

  5. The release

    The anger turns into teasing, then laughter, then the highest energy of the video.

  6. Come-down and punchline

    Everything slows down, then the duck keels over and ends on its back with its legs up. The title card holds on that frame.

Each emotion
