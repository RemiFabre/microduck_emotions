The picks

YES (LB), 1.5 s

motion: one nod: head_pitch 0 → +0.7 over 0.25 s from 0.05 s, hold 0.1 s, back over 0.35 s (the joint bottoms out at ~0.45 s, 38°). Neck 0, yaw 0.

sound: one quack at 0.45 s, 250 Hz falling 3 semitones over 0.3 s. Robot wav sounds/robot/yes_a.wav.

Choices: one nod or two (yes_double)? flat, falling or the bank's greet? all 12 pairs

NO (RB), 1.8 s

motion: one head shake, yaw only: +0.55 at 0.35 s, −0.55 at 0.85 s, 0.25 s fades, centre at 1.10 s (joint ±0.4, lags 0.25 s).

sound: "no" at 0.60 s (250 Hz, flat), "ah" at 1.10 s a fourth lower, falling. Robot wavs no_a (this), no_b (nasal, sulky), no_c (drawn-out "aaah"): the robot picks one at random per play.

Choices: interval of the second note (−3 / −5 / −7 semitones), synth or the bank's voice, one shake or two. all 18 pairs

ANGRY (X), 2.6 s

motion: beak-up glare (head_pitch −0.35), four snaps at 0.30 / 0.90 / 1.50 / 2.00 s: yaw +0.7 / −0.7 / +0.7 / centre, a short head jab (+0.45), a small fast bow pulse (+0.14) on the first three. Neck 0. Beak forced wide 0.30–0.70 s so the leash drops.

sound: four hard barks (300 Hz, click attack, rasp), the last held and chopped. Robot wavs angry_a (barks) and angry_b (a growl swelling into the same barks).

finding: a body-pose "bow" above ~0.2 folds the whole duck forward (trunk −20..−37°, 5–14 cm drift) = the head-forward mass that makes the real robot walk. Only small fast pulses are clean; body z does nothing on the stand net.

Choices: is 0.4 s of wide beak enough for the leash? scold (this) or menace (crouch_shake_lunge, growl)? all 12 pairs

EXCITED (DPad-Down), 3.4 s

motion: six accelerating head swings ±0.6 (extremes at 0.40 / 0.95 / 1.45 / 1.90 / 2.30 / 2.65 s), the beak climbing from −0.15 to −0.70, a body bob (+0.12 bow pulse, 12–16 mm dip) on each swing, a bow flourish at the end. Neck 0.

sound: the bank's own chirps in rising pitch order (236 → 300 Hz), one per swing. Robot wav excited_a.wav.

Choices: bank chirps (this), synth climb (X1) or the giddy "wak" (X3)? bigger body (excited_hops, 30 mm dips)? all 14 pairs

PLAY DEAD (DPad-Left), 7.5 s

motion: sit + alarm on the press (head snaps up); 1.0–2.0 s the head goes back (beak to the sky) and to the side (yaw +0.6); 2.2 s: robot.soften (gain dies over 1 s, torque off); the duck keels over backwards on the weight of its head (tips at 3.0 s, flat on its back at 4.8 s, head on the side); 4.8–6.6 s the death quack with the beak open 0.3.

sound: bank alarm, silence, a synth glide 260 → 120 Hz with a 5 Hz wobble that dies. Robot wav play_dead_a.wav.

probe: sit + soften does NOT fall (the seat is stable limp); the head thrown back is the lever (6.5 rad/s); cutting the rise short falls harder (9–11 rad/s); a seated lean lands propped at 65°.

Questions: may it end limp (Start = the way up)? the head cannot straighten after the cut (it goes to the side before); on the real robot the jaw is gated on "driving", so the death quack will play with a shut beak; test the fall on a mat. probe table + 7 videos

CLOSED-BEAK QUACK (scene only, {"express":"closed_quack"}), 2.2 s

motion: a small yaw shake (−0.25 at 0.6 s, +0.25 at 1.05 s), beak shut throughout (the leash stays in).

sound: two low nasal buzzes at 0.50 / 0.95 s, low-passed at 900 Hz (the muffling is in the wav). Robot wav closed_a.wav.

Alternatives: the curious chirps with a roll wobble and the beak shut (friendlier), a muffled "mmh?". 4 cards
