YES, FAST (L3), 1.5 s

motion: the same nod as yes.

sound: a curt 0.18 s "wak" falling a fourth (Rémi's second yes: the quick one for the yes / no / yes exchange). Robot wav yes_fast_a.wav.

NO (RB), 1.8 s

motion: one head shake, yaw only: +0.55 at 0.35 s, −0.55 at 0.85 s, 0.25 s fades, centre at 1.10 s (joint ±0.4, lags 0.25 s).

sound: "no" at 0.60 s (250 Hz, flat), "ah" at 1.10 s a fourth lower, falling. Robot wavs no_a (this), no_b (nasal, sulky), no_c (drawn-out "aaah"): the robot picks one at random per play.

Choices: interval of the second note (−3 / −5 / −7 semitones), synth or the bank's voice, one shake or two. all 18 pairs

ANGRY (X), 2.6 s

motion: beak-up glare (head_pitch −0.35), four snaps at 0.30 / 0.90 / 1.50 / 2.00 s: yaw +0.7 / −0.7 / +0.7 / centre, a short head jab (+0.45), a small fast bow pulse (+0.14) on the first three. Neck 0. Beak forced wide 0.30–0.70 s so the leash drops.

sound: four hard barks (300 Hz, click attack, rasp), the last held and chopped. Robot wavs angry_a (barks) and angry_b (a growl swelling into the same barks).

finding: a body-pose "bow" above ~0.2 folds the whole duck forward (trunk −20..−37°, 5–14 cm drift) = the head-forward mass that makes the real robot walk. Only small fast pulses are clean; body z does nothing on the stand net.

Choices: is 0.4 s of wide beak enough for the leash? scold (this) or menace (crouch_shake_lunge, growl)? all 12 pairs

EXCITED (DPad-Down), 3.4 s

motion: six accelerating head swings ±0.6 (extremes at 0.40 / 0.95 / 1.45 / 1.90 / 2.30 / 2.65 s), the beak climbing from −0.15 to −0.70, a body bob (+0.12 bow pulse, 12–16 mm dip) on each swing, a bow flourish at the end. Neck 0.

sound: X1 synth climb (Rémi's pick): each quack higher, shorter and rising more. Robot wav excited_a.wav.

all 14 pairs

PLAY DEAD (DPad-Left), 7.5 s, v3 "the dead animal"

motion: sit + alarm on the press while the head snaps up and turns hard to the side (yaw 1.0 over 0.5 s); 0.4–1.0 s the head goes back; 1.4 s: the three head servos hang free and every leg joint is driven to zero over 1.5 s (a new runtime call, robot.poseJoints): the seated duck rolls flat onto its back (tips at 2.0 s, still at 3.0 s), the limp head on the side; 3.6 s: the legs come up (hips ±1.0, knees ±1.5 over 1 s), the body unmoved; 5.0–6.8 s the death quack, beak 0.3 (the jaw stays powered). No relax, no re-init: the robot holds the dead pose until your Start.

sound: alarm, silence, the falling synth glide with a dying wobble. Robot wav play_dead_a.wav.

probe (27 recipes): every "legs to zero" recipe rolls the duck onto its back; the slower the leg ramp the softer (1.5 s: 7.5 rad/s, 0.5 s: 9.6); gain 100/160/200 make no difference; straight to legs-up is marginal (rocks or ends propped); two stages is the softest and lands flat. The unpowered head ends folded back on the shoulder shell (own weight only). Head roll servo kept on (off: the head hangs crooked).

Mat first (7.45 rad/s, head 1.0 m/s; a 2 s leg ramp would be softer). Options: legs flat instead of up (pd_v3_flat), four head servos off (pd_v3_dead_off4). Check the head cable where the limp head rests on the shoulder. probe table + videos (v3, v2, v1)
